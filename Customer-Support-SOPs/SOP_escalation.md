# SOP: Escalation Matrix

When a VA cannot resolve an issue within defined steps, escalate according to this matrix.

Priority levels

- P1: Service down / payment failure affecting many customers — escalate to Tier 2 immediately.
- P2: Individual customer outage / lost order — escalate to Tier 1 after 30 minutes of attempted troubleshooting.
- P3: Minor issues / feature requests — log and schedule for product review.

Escalation steps

1. Flag ticket with label (P1/P2/P3).
2. Add summary and steps taken to the ticket.
3. Notify the on-call specialist via Slack/Email with a short summary and ticket link.

Example escalation message

"Hi @OnCall, I'm escalating ticket #123 (P2). Customer: Jane Doe. Issue: missing shipment. Steps tried: verified tracking, requested proof of delivery. Please advise next steps."
